### Procedure

#### Marking of the area:<br>
- Mark the dimensions of the foundation area diagonally and rectangularly. (2700 mm × 2400 mm)<br>

#### Setting up of the basic arrangements of the formwork:<br>
- Bring the form floor panels, form floor corners, and waler clamps in the lab<br>
- Utilize the waler clamps to connect the form floor panels to the form floor corners<br>

#### Attachment of the horizontal supports:<br>
- Attach the waler pipes to the form floor panel by using the waler clamps<br>
- Attach the steel walers to the waler pipes by using steel waler connectors<br>

#### Provision of the vertical supports:<br>
- Attach the CT props to the steel walers by using the head and foot adapters<br>

#### Completion of the formwork:<br>
- Repeat steps 2 to 4 for the other three sides of the formwork to complete a consistent and stable formwork<br>
